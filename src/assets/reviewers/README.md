from Unsplash (free under the Unsplash License), cropped to 128×128 on the
face. Named after the reviewer they stand in for; a reviewer with no file
shows their initial instead.

⚠️ These are real people's photos next to invented reviews of real firms.
Fine for the team's mock; replace or remove before anything leaves the team.
