A brief reaches the partner

  1. 1Brief card. Title, scope clamped to two lines, and Read brief, which opens the full brief dialog. Budget and industry stay in the panel.
  2. 2Your quote. Price, weeks, note. "Waiting on their decision" shows until the business shows interest.
  3. 3Brief as a link field. Plain text led by a file icon, gray fill on hover. No underline, no chevron.
lead = { source: 'connect', company: null, // until shortlisted stage: 'Open', closed: null, hireRequest: null, }

No manual leads.Leads come from Connect briefs, or from the firm's CRM while sync is on. Adding one by hand belongs in the CRM, so there's one copy.

The business shows interest

  1. 1Thread event shared. The lead now has a company: name, contact, email, phone.
  2. 2Match question. If the lead's email domain matches one of your customers, we ask if they're the same company. It sits under the company name, and goes away once someone answers or the lead closes. Only Owners and Sales see it.
  3. 3Yes, link adds the Customer field, logs "Customer: Not set → Halcyon", toasts "Linked to Halcyon". Not them hides the box; a win makes a new customer.

The partner picks Won

  1. 1Won stays in the stage menu, so the rule shows up where a partner would look for it.

A partner can't set Won on a Connect lead.The business is on Connect, and the hire is where they agree the terms. A partner-set Won would leave their project saying nobody is hired.

CRM leads have a read-only stage badge. Won comes from the CRM.

04
Partner

The request is out

  1. 1Thread line hire-sent. Withdrawn and declined add their own lines.
  2. 2Panel row while the request is out. Withdraw acts at once with an Undo toast, no confirm dialog.
  3. 3Leads list: the Stage cell appends "· Hire sent".
  • The stage doesn't change while waiting.
  • A pending request blocks a second one.
  • Lost, Junk or Closed withdraws it automatically.
  • After a decline the partner can send again.

No "Won · waiting" stage.It called a lead Won before it was, and would count as Won wherever stages are summed. The request is a status beside the stage.

Business

The business hires

  1. 1A one-row frappe-ui Alert, like the link requests on the partner's Customers list: who asked and when, with Review on the right. Review opens the hire dialog.
  2. 2Opened from a request, the footer answers it: Decline request (subtle) beside Hire, and the × closes without deciding. Decline clears the request with an Undo toast; the firm stays in Interested and its thread gets hire-declined. From any other row the footer is Cancel and Hire.
  3. 3Requested marks the firm that asked, sorted first so the bar has a row to point at. Every row's Hire is subtle.
  4. 4The partner's name is in the title, which wraps for long legal names. The button just says Hire. It stays enabled; without the box ticked, "Agree to the terms to hire" appears on the left of the footer, so nothing moves.
  5. 5One question once they've hired. Four one-tap reasons, or Other with a line of text. Skip or closing loses nothing; the hire is already done.
  6. 6Lands on step 2. The quotes table goes away and the partner moves to the rail. Toast: "Tridots Tech is your partner".
choosePartner(project, partner) // clears hireRequest declineHire(project) // clears hireRequest project.hireRequestAt // for "· 2:10 pm"

The partner doesn't confirm the hire.Their quote is the offer, and the other terms are in their partner agreement. A second yes would leave the project in limbo.

No line telling the business others will be told.They'd expect it, and there's nothing for them to do about it.

Records

The win

  1. 1Stage is a badge once Won, so a hired lead can't be reopened by a slip. The lead leaves Open leads; it stays under All leads or a Won filter.
  2. 2Customer field with the customer's avatar. Opens the customer page.
  3. 3Footer replaces the reply box. View in Customers opens the customer on Messages (?tab=messages).
  4. 4Hired another partner. Thread line hired-other, starting "Hire request closed" if one was out.
  5. 5Lost, reason "Lost to competitor". No customer is made.
  6. 6The thread closes for both sides.
  • Matched (auto or "Yes, link"): the lead id is added to that customer. No new record.
  • No match: a new customer, as shown.
  • CRM lead: the same, from the CRM's Won, without thread events.
  • CRM reopens it: the customer the win created is removed if it has no sites and no other leads. Otherwise only this project comes off it.

Two records, linked.The lead keeps the bid history the pipeline and win rate need. The customer is where work continues. This is Frappe CRM's lead and deal shape.

No redirect.Opening a won lead shows the lead. Landing on a different kind of record than the one clicked was confusing in review.

Work continues on the customer

BusinessPartner

The partner code links the customer

  1. 1Partner code in the header with a copy icon only ("Copy code", toast "Code copied"). The empty list shows it too.
  2. 2Alert above the table, owners only. Requests aren't rows, so every row opens a customer page.
  3. 3Review dialog steps through one request at a time. Fixed body height so buttons don't move. Each action toasts with Undo; it closes after the last.
  4. 4Who pays is a ghost select for the owner while the customer pays, is linked and hasn't churned. "You" opens the existing Switch to Partner pays confirmation.

Credit and MRR only from here.Won is a record of what happened, not a claim on money. That's why CRM wins need no signature from the business.

Planned: a business hired in Connect is approved without review when it links. Not in the prototype.

Open questions

  • Connect leads with CRM sync on. Their stage is read-only, so no hire request. Should Won still send it?
  • Switching back to Customer pays. Not in the select yet.
  • One partner code or one per project. Which does Frappe Cloud issue?
  • Merging customers after an unanswered match. Not designed.
  • Change partner. What happens to sessions already run, money already paid, and the old partner's say.
